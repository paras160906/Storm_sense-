### 3.3 Reproduction Verdict & Diagnostic Findings
* **Verdict**: **REPRODUCED (within 0.61%, well under the 1% target threshold).**
* **Root Cause Diagnosis**:
  1. **Dataset Provenance**: The model was trained directly on the real August 18/19 dataset (specifically August 18 for training and the first half of August 19 for validation). It was never trained on the 45 corrupted synthetic samples in `backend/stormsense/dataset/`.
  2. **Loss Function Discrepancy**: The training run utilized standard `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with `pos_weight = 100.0` over lightning and radar intensity heads:
     $$\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^T \left( \text{BCE}(\hat{y}_{t}^{\text{light}}, y_t^{\text{light}}; w=100) + \text{BCE}(\hat{y}_{t}^{\text{inten}}, y_t^{\text{inten}}; w=100) \right)$$
     In contrast, [`backend/stormsense/stormsense/losses.py`](file:///c:/Users/Paras/Downloads/StormSense%20(2)/StormSense/backend/stormsense/stormsense/losses.py) subsequently introduced heteroscedastic uncertainty loss:
     $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{hetero}} = \frac{\text{BCE}}{\sigma^2} + \log \sigma^2$$
     When executed with the new code path, large learned log-variances drive the loss negative ($-1.9817$). The checkpoint's recorded metric space reflects the original unweighted variance BCE path.
  3. **Near-Zero CSI Skill**: Both the recorded CSI ($0.00077$) and reproduced CSI ($0.00115$) confirm severe over-prediction bias caused by the aggressive `pos_weight=100.0` multiplier on sparse storm pixels.

### Strategic Recommendation
1. **No Retraining on Current Data**: Retraining on a 2-day dataset with 51 training samples and 63 validation samples cannot produce an operationally valid or generalizable nowcaster. It would merely re-fit noise and diurnal correlation.
2. **Prototype Positioning**: StormSense must be presented strictly as a **multimodal pipeline proof-of-concept demonstration**:
   * The engineering architecture (INSAT regridding + DWR polar-to-Cartesian scatter interpolation + ERA5 bilinear spatial interpolation + OpenWeatherMap lightning gridding + ConvLSTM/AxialAttention network) is verified and functional end-to-end.
   * Model outputs are uncalibrated and lack held-out empirical verification.
3. **Data Acquisition Roadmap**: To achieve Tier A operational readiness, a minimum of **20–30 convective thunderstorm days** across pre-monsoon and monsoon seasons (May–September) must be ingested from MOSDAC INSAT-3DR and IMD Cherrapunji DWR archives before initiating production training runs.
